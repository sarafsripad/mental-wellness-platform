Privacy by design

Names and villages are encrypted in a separate identity store and are never shown again. Everything else is saved as coded answers, with no free text. In offline mode, unsynced visits are kept on this device encrypted under your PIN.

Safety reminder

This tool does not diagnose. Urgent concerns require trained human support and the approved local referral protocol.
